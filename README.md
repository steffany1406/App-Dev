# 🎯 Hunter x Hunter

## **Overview**
*Hunter x Hunter* is a Japanese anime and manga created by **Yoshihiro Togashi**.  
It follows **Gon Freecss**, a boy who discovers that his father—whom he thought was dead—is actually a legendary Hunter. Gon sets out on a journey to become a Hunter himself and find his father.

---

## **Main Characters**
- **Gon Freecss**: The optimistic protagonist with incredible potential.  
- **Killua Zoldyck**: Gon’s best friend, heir to a family of assassins, skilled and cunning.  
- **Kurapika**: The last surviving member of the Kurta Clan, driven by revenge.  
- **Leorio Paradinight**: Aspiring doctor, motivated by compassion and loyalty.  
- **Hisoka Morow**: A mysterious, dangerous magician with unpredictable motives.  

---

## **Key Themes**
1. **Friendship & Loyalty** – The bond between Gon and Killua is central.  
2. **Ambition & Growth** – Each character pursues their own goals, often at great cost.  
3. **Morality & Power** – The series explores the blurred lines between good and evil.

## **Major Arcs**
| **Arc** | **Focus** | **Highlights** |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Hunter Exam | Gon meets Killua, Kurapika, Leorio | Tests of skill and survival |
| Heaven’s Arena | Gon & Killua train | Introduction to Nen |
| Yorknew City | Kurapika vs Phantom Troupe | Dark, intense battles |
| Greed Island | Gon & Killua enter a game | Strategy and teamwork |
| Chimera Ant | Humanity vs Chimera Ants | Epic, emotional arc |
| Election Arc | Choosing a new Hunter Chairman | Gon’s reunion with Ging |

---

## **Fun Fact**
- The *Chimera Ant Arc* is often considered one of the **greatest arcs in anime history** due to its depth, themes, and emotional impact.
